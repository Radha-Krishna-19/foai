Smart Traffic Management - conventional city vs ours

Same cars, same traffic, same accident. Left: a city as it works today. Right: our multi-agent system.

Problem: fixed-timing lights ignore real queues, drivers only learn about an accident when they reach it, and ambulances wait at red lights. Our solution: junction agents give green to the busier road (Max-Pressure), the traffic centre alerts cars so they re-plan with A*, and the ambulance requests a green corridor.

What is happening

  1. Every car runs A* at the start and drives its cheapest route (dashed).
  2. At t = s the black road(s) close.
  3. Conventional: nobody is told. A driver only changes route when the closed road is the very next one. Lights switch on a fixed timer, so cars wait even when the other road is empty, and the ambulance queues at red lights.
  4. Ours: the traffic centre broadcasts the accident; each car checks “is it on my route?” and runs A* again if so. Junctions give green to the road with more waiting cars (Max-Pressure). The ambulance sends PREEMPT_OVERRIDE, junctions turn green for it and resume normal control after it passes.
