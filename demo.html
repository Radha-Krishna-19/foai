The problem

City traffic wastes time in three ways. Fixed-timing signals give the same green to an empty road as to a packed one. Drivers only learn about an accident when they run into it. And an ambulance sits at red lights like everyone else.
Our solution: every junction and car is an agent. Junctions pick green by the queues they see (Max-Pressure). The traffic centre broadcasts the accident and cars re-plan with A*. The ambulance asks junctions for a green corridor.

What is happening

  1. Every car runs A* at the start and drives the cheapest route (dashed line).
  2. At t = s the accident closes the road(s) in black.
  3. Conventional: nobody is told. A driver only changes route when the closed road is the very next one. Lights switch on a fixed timer, so cars wait even when the other road is empty. The ambulance queues at red lights.
  4. Ours: the traffic centre broadcasts the accident; each car checks "is it on my route?" and, if so, runs A* again. Junction agents give green to the road with more waiting cars (Max-Pressure). The ambulance sends PREEMPT_OVERRIDE, junctions turn green for it and switch back after it passes.
