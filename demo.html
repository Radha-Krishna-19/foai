Smart Traffic Management - conventional city vs ours

Same random traffic, same accident, same vehicles. Left: a city as it works today. Right: our multi-agent system.

Problem: fixed-timing lights ignore real queues, drivers only learn about an accident when they reach it, and ambulances wait at red lights. Our solution: junction agents give green to the busier road (Max-Pressure), the traffic centre alerts cars so they re-plan with A*, and the ambulance requests a green corridor.

What is happening

  1. About 160 random cars (blue-grey moving, orange stopped) fill the city. They are not tracked: they only make traffic.
  2. You press Send Car or Send Ambulance. It gets a random start and destination and runs A* to find the shortest route, drawn as a thick coloured line (arrows show the direction).
  3. At t = s the black road(s) close. Conventional: nobody is told; a driver changes route only when the closed road is the very next one (dashed line = original route). Lights switch on a fixed timer, so queues grow even when the other road is empty, and the ambulance waits at red.
  4. Ours: the traffic centre broadcasts the accident; cars with the road on their route run A* again. Junctions give green to the road with more waiting cars (Max-Pressure). The ambulance sends PREEMPT_OVERRIDE and junctions turn green for it.
